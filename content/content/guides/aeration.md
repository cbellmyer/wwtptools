<div id="overview" class="story-chapter-label">[ Overview ]</div>

Aeration is the single largest energy cost at most activated sludge plants and the variable operators adjust most frequently. Getting it right means understanding what the biology needs, what the equipment can deliver, and how those two numbers diverge during load events and seasonal changes.
